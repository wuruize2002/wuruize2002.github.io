## Research | 科研

### Reconstructing Missing Block-Level Morphological Evidence to Support Urban Renewal-Unit Delineation

**Accepted · Journal of Asian Architecture and Building Engineering (JAABE)**  
Developed **Morpho-GNN**, a morphology-aware graph neural network that reconstructs incomplete block-level urban-form evidence while preserving neighbourhood continuity and transition structure for urban-renewal analysis.  
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1080/13467581.2026.2715947)

### Urban Blocks Regionalization by Integrating Spatial Proximity and Morphological Similarity

**Accepted · CAADRIA 2026**  
Proposed a dual-channel **Morph-GNN** that jointly models spatial adjacency and morphological similarity to produce contiguous, internally coherent urban-form regions from 4,364 blocks in Nanjing.  
[CUMINCAD paper page](https://cumincad.org/cgi-bin/works/Show?caadria2026_351)

### Graph-Based Data Model for Micro-Spatial Diagnosis in Historic Urban Districts: A Case Study of Shanghai's Wukang Road District

**Accepted · CDRF 2026**  
Developed a building-space heterogeneous graph for historic-district diagnosis, connecting morphology and external-space evidence to support preservation-first and low-intervention regeneration decisions in Shanghai's Wukang Road district.
